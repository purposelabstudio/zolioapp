Privacy and data

Your notebook starts on your phone.

Write offline. Choose an optional personal-cloud backup when you'd like to keep a restorable copy.

Writing without a Zolio account

Your notebook is stored locally on your device. You can write, mark moods and keep habits without signing up for a Zolio account. Purchases use your platform store account, and cloud backup uses the provider account you choose.

A copy you can restore

Optional backup saves a snapshot to your chosen supported provider. Android supports Google Drive; iPhone supports Google Drive or iCloud Drive. Creating backups is a Plus feature. A saved copy can still be restored even if you no longer have Plus.

Restoring a copy replaces the notebook on that phone. Check that a backup completed before relying on it; a connection, provider access and time for the backup to finish are needed.

What app lock does

PIN lock controls opening the app. App lock is different from encrypting stored files. The privacy policy says Zolio does not add separate end-to-end encryption to backup files; App Lock does not encrypt a cloud copy.

Usage and crash reporting

Zolio includes usage and crash-reporting services. Structured analytics events exclude notebook text and titles. Service behaviour can depend on the platform, build and settings. The privacy policy explains the services and data practices.

Deleting writing and older backups

Earlier backups can still contain words that you later delete or let go of in the app. Manage those backup copies separately. Restoring an older copy can bring older content back.

Read the declarations

The policy and store declarations cover more than notebook storage, including measurement services and data categories.
