Before you download

The practical things you might want to know first.

Can I use Zolio on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Zolio has iPhone and Android apps. Store availability and paid options can vary by platform and region.

What's included in Free and Zolio Plus?

Core writing, mood marks and PIN lock are free to use, with an included set of habits, to-dos, paper styles, fonts and a writing sound.

Optional Zolio Plus lets you add photos and short voice notes within daily attachment limits, create personal-cloud backups, browse older calendar months and annual views, and choose more customisation options.

See current pricing and available options in your app store and the in-app Plus screen. You can restore a previously saved backup even if you no longer have Plus.

Can I write offline, without an account?

Core writing, mood and habit features work offline without a Zolio account. Store purchases and optional cloud backup use their respective accounts and need a connection for online operations.

Can I add photos and voice notes?

Adding new photo and voice attachments requires Plus and has daily attachment limits. Zolio is a journal with media alongside writing, not an unlimited photo or audio archive.

How do backups work?

Optional personal-cloud backup saves a copy of your notebook to the supported provider you choose. You can restore a saved copy later; restoring it replaces the notebook on that phone. Read how backup works.

What if I haven't written for a while?

Start with today. You can also keep one note for a span of days rather than reconstructing each day separately.

How is my notebook protected?

Your notebook starts on your device, with PIN app lock available. A lock and encryption are different protections. Optional backups and diagnostics have their own boundaries. Read about your notebook and data, including what happens to older backups after deletion.

What is Oli?

Oli offers local pattern observations and questions from notebook activity signals, with evidence you can inspect or disagree with.
