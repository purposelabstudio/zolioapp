The story behind Zolio

You don't always know which details you'll want to remember.

Room for an ordinary day

Not every day comes with a story ready to tell. Sometimes there's a good lunch, a long wait, a sentence from someone you care about. A journal gives those small things somewhere to stay.

Zolio is built around the idea that a short entry counts. You can write a line, keep a mood or habit mark beside it, and leave the page there. When you return, you can start with today.

Writing, then looking back

Keeping a page is only part of it. Months later, the details can be what brings a day back: where you went, what you noticed, what mattered then.

That's why Zolio brings dated pages, reflection prompts and ways to revisit earlier writing into the same app. Pages holds everyday entries. Pause gives you room to follow a thought. Rhythm shows habit activity, and Moments helps you look back.

From PurposeLab Studio

PurposeLab Studio is an indie studio based in India, making focused everyday tools for people worldwide. The studio's approach is to build useful software that respects people's time and stays out of their way.

For Zolio, that means keeping core writing offline and giving you space to decide how much of a day to keep.
