Considering Zolio?

Keep the context
attached to the day.

A page, a mood and a habit mark can tell you different things. Zolio keeps them together, with ways to reflect and read back.

Begin with the entry

Write about today, then add a mood or habit mark if it's useful. Pause has prompts and unsent letters when you want to explore a thought further.

Read more than a date

Use Moments, tags, bookmarks or search to follow a detail back to its page. After a gap, you can start today or save one note across several days.

Write without a Zolio account

Core writing works offline on your phone. An optional personal-cloud backup saves a copy you can restore.

How it fits alongside other journals

Day One emphasises rich media and writing across devices. Daylio centres mood and activity check-ins. Diarium offers a journal across desktop and mobile platforms.

Zolio is worth trying if you want everyday writing, mood and habit context, and reflection in one notebook. Start with a dated entry and see how you like returning to it.
